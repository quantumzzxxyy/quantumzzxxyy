Systemic Failures in Agentic Architectures

Why LLM Agents Fail, and What Control Theory Teaches Us About Fixing Them

by Melvyn White

The transition from LLMs as chatbots to autonomous agents introduces fundamental failure modes: unbounded execution, error propagation, and lack of verification. This paper applies control theory—poles, BIBO stability, and feedback loops—to understand why agents fail and how to fix them.
